Holding the Scene

  1. Third-person, past participle. “Ser Aldric drew his blade” — not “I draw my blade.”
  2. Let every player read the room. React to what is true, not what you wish were true.
  3. Collab scenes and quiet moments are as welcome as great battles.
  4. Ask leave before touching another player’s property, castles, army, or family.
