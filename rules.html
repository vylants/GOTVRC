Rules & Joining

We Do Not Sow

The laws of our realm are few and iron-given. Obey them, and the story sings. Break them, and the wall falls. All are expected to read the terms below before swearing fealty.

How to Join

  1. Step into our hall. Join the Discord server via the roleplay hub and introduce yourself to the community.
  2. Read the lore. Understand the state of the realm and choose where your story begins.
  3. Swear a character. Submit an application naming your character, house alignment, and backstory.
  4. Await the judgment. Moderation reviews every petition. You will be approved, tasked with edits, or offered counsel.
  5. Take up your sword. Once approved, weave your tale with the other players of the realm.

The Laws of the Realm

  1. Respect every player. Out-of-character friction has no place at court. Disputes go before the moderators.
  2. No godmodding. You may not control another player’s character or decide the outcome of contested action.
  3. Keep OOC out of IC. Game terms, dice, and out-of-character talk never bleed into the in-character scene.
  4. Power stays earned. Major victories, realm-changing events, and plotlines require moderation approval.
  5. Respect the rating. Mature themes are allowed within the community rating. Flashes of shock value are not storytelling.
  6. Honor the timeline. Follow the official state of the realm. Retcons and time-travelers earn a clean sword to the neck.
  7. One character, one truth. Keep identities consistent and avoid duplicating canon figures without permission.
