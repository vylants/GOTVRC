Welcome to the Realm

A Game of Thrones roleplay told by its players — courts and keeps, feuds and alliances, and the long road between them. Everything begins with a seat in our Discord hall.

How to Join & Apply

  1. Join the Discord. That is the door to the realm — every scene, event, and announcement lives there.
  2. Read the rules & lore. The expectations and the state of the world are posted in the server's welcome channels.
  3. Submit your application. Post it in the server's application channel and a moderator will review your petition.
  4. Take your place. Once approved, choose your house, claim your character, and write your first scene.
The Gate Is Open

Applications are handled inside our Discord server. Join us and a moderator will greet you at the gate.
