Lore & World

We Do Not Sow

Westeros and the wild lands beyond are living in our era. Below is the canon of our realm — write it down, weave it into your story, and let the gods (the moderators) judge the rest.

The State of the Realm

Our story begins in the waning years following Robert’s Rebellion. The Iron Throne has its claimant, the Great Houses hold their ancient seats, and winter is creeping closer than any maester would care to admit. Who holds the realm, and how the Long Night breaks, is for our players to write together.

Geography of the Seven Kingdoms

The North

A vast, cold land ruled from Winterfell by House Stark. Marked by vast forests, walled keeps, and the frozen Wall at its edge.

The Riverlands

Crossroads of the realm, soaked in the blood of a hundred wars. Riverrun, the Twins, and the burned lands between.

The Westerlands

Golden hills and rich mines beneath Casterly Rock. House Lannister holds purses heavier than any crown.

The Reach

The breadbasket of Westeros. Highgarden rises in a garden of plenty, and the Tyrells grow strong.

The Stormlands

Storm-lashed coasts and the great keep of Storm’s End, held by the fury of House Baratheon.

The Vale

Mountains ring the Vale of Arryn, defended by the unassailable Eyrie and the falcon’s honor.

Dorne

The southernmost principality, sandy and sun-baked. Dornishmen bow to no one but their own princes.

The Iron Islands

Storm-wracked rocks off the western coast, home to the ironborn who take what they do not sow.

Faiths & Powers

The Faith of the Seven

The High Septon and his maestors guide the True Faith across the Seven Kingdoms, honoring the Father, Mother, Warrior, Maiden, Crone, Smith, and Stranger.

The Old Gods

Heart trees carved with watching faces hold court in the North. The gods of the forest remember.

The Drowned God

What is dead may never die. The ironborn kneel to a god who died beneath the waves.

Dragons & Magic

Whether dragons still dance above the Narrow Sea is asked in hushed halls. Sorcery walks quietly in the shadows of our era.

Word of the realm

What men know divides more than what they do not.
